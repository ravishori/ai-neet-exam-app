# Glossary
## AI NEET Exam App
### Enterprise Project Glossary

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the terminology used throughout the AI NEET Exam App.

Cursor AI and contributors should use these definitions consistently.

Avoid creating multiple meanings for the same term.

When uncertain, prefer these definitions.

---

# Academic Terms

## NEET

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate).

Primary examination supported by this platform.

---

## Subject

Highest academic grouping.

Examples

- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology

---

## Chapter

A subdivision of a subject.

Example

Subject

Physics

↓

Chapter

Laws of Motion

---

## Topic

Subdivision of a chapter.

Example

Chapter

Electrostatics

↓

Topic

Electric Field

---

## Concept

Smallest academic learning unit.

Examples

Ohm's Law

Kirchhoff's Rule

Hardy-Weinberg Principle

Chemical Equilibrium

Concepts are the foundation of learning and knowledge mapping.

---

# Assessment Terms

## Question

A single assessment item presented to the student.

---

## Question Bank

Collection of questions available in the system.

---

## PYQ

Previous Year Question.

Represents an actual examination question from previous NEET exams.

---

## Difficulty

Relative complexity of a question.

Typical values

Easy

Medium

Hard

---

## Explanation

Educational content describing why an answer is correct.

---

## Option

One possible answer in a multiple-choice question.

---

## Correct Answer

The officially verified answer.

---

## Solution

Complete reasoning used to solve a question.

---

# Learning Terms

## Practice Session

A collection of questions attempted by a student.

---

## Revision

Targeted review of previously studied concepts.

---

## Flashcard

A lightweight learning object used for active recall.

---

## Bookmark

A question saved by the student for later review.

---

## Note

Personal annotation created by the student.

---

## Performance

Student learning metrics.

Examples

Accuracy

Speed

Consistency

Weak Areas

Strong Areas

---

# AI Terms

## AI Explanation

An explanation generated with AI assistance.

Must always be educationally accurate.

---

## Recommendation

An AI-generated suggestion.

Examples

Questions

Concepts

Revision

Study Plan

---

## Knowledge Unit

The smallest structured educational entity extracted during ingestion.

Knowledge Units form the foundation for downstream learning assets.

---

## Knowledge Graph

Graph representing relationships between concepts, chapters, questions, and educational content.

---

# Content Management Terms

## Ingestion

Process of importing educational material into the platform.

Examples

Books

PDFs

Question Papers

Notes

---

## Document

A source file imported into the platform.

---

## Page

A single page within a document.

---

## Visual Asset

Extracted image, figure, diagram, graph, table, or illustration.

---

## OCR

Optical Character Recognition.

Used when text must be extracted from images.

---

## Layout Element

A detected structural element on a document page.

Examples

Paragraph

Heading

Table

Figure

Diagram
